eScoresheet: for referees

Some clubs now write the scoresheet on a tablet or laptop. You referee as always, and the scorer records; this page says what changes for you.

For SVRZ games only. Mobiliar Cup and 1st League games are still scored on paper.

Before the match

  1. Check the rosters on the scorer's device. “Show rosters” lists both teams with numbers, licences, liberos, captain and bench officials.
  2. The app takes your names from the schedule. Give the scorer your email address; the scoresheet goes to it as a PDF after the match.
  3. The scorer records the coin toss. The captain and the coach of each team then sign on the screen or on their own phone, and set 1 can only start once all four have signed.

The referee tablet (optional)

You can follow the match on your own phone or tablet. The scorer opens “Connect tablets” in the menu, you scan the code and enter the PIN; without a code it works through referee.openvolley.app. You see the score and sets, the serving team, both line-ups with the rotation, time-outs, substitutions and sanctions, and time-outs and intervals show a countdown. The tablet only displays; the scorer still records.

During the match

When the scorer records a time-out, a substitution or a sanction, it appears on the referee tablet in a window that closes by itself after 10 seconds. If the scorer needs you, they tap the bell, and your tablet shows “Scorer needs attention” and vibrates where it can.

When you take a rally back, for a wrong server, wrong positions or an illegal substitution, the scorer picks the score where it went wrong under Match › “Go back”, and who gets the point and the serve. The app writes the remark on the scoresheet itself. This works in the running set; the scorer corrects an earlier set afterwards and tells you.

If the internet drops, the scorer simply keeps scoring; the data goes to the server once the connection is back.

After the match

  1. The two captains sign first, then the assistant scorer (if entered), the scorer, the 2nd and last the 1st referee. You can sign on the screen, or tap “Sign on phone”, scan the code and sign on your own phone.
  2. The scorer changes remarks with “Edit remarks”. A referee coach signs with “Add a signature to the remarks”; their signature is printed in the remarks.
  3. Then the scorer taps “Confirm and approve”, and the match is done.

How you get the scoresheet

A few minutes after the approval, the scoresheet goes by email as a PDF to the scorer, to you and to each team's contact. You get it at the address the scorer entered with your name. The scorer can also show it to you, print it or save it as a PDF (“Scoresheet” › “Preview”, “Print” or “Save PDF”), before and after the approval. If no email arrives, write to support@openvolley.app with the game number and date; we send you the scoresheet.

No account needed

As a referee you need no OpenVolley account. You sign by hand, on the screen or on your own phone, and the scoresheet goes to the email address you give the scorer.

Help: the “?” on the referee tablet, all questions and a video on www.openvolley.app/en/support, questions to support@openvolley.app. Licence numbers, signatures and dates of birth on the scoresheet are never public (privacy policy).
