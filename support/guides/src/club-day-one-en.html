eScoresheet: day one for clubs

From your first home game you write the scoresheet on a tablet or laptop instead of on paper. These two pages say what scorers and club managers do for that on day one.

What you need

The scorer's table needs one device: a tablet in landscape or a laptop, or if need be a phone held upright. The app runs in the browser at app.openvolley.app; if you would rather install it, the desktop and Android apps are on get.openvolley.app. At the first open it asks how you use OpenVolley, and you choose “Swiss”.

You need internet only at the start, because the page has to load once and the schedule needs a connection. After that you can score with no network at all, and the data goes to the server once the connection is back. Plan one practice evening before it counts: a test match has invented teams and is never saved on the server.

Scorers: day one

At home, before the first match

  1. Open the app and tap “?”, then “Your first match”. The tour takes you through a test match, from the rosters and the coin toss through set 1 to set 5 and the signatures. If you would rather watch, the same exists as a video (7:29).
  2. If your club has decided that you score with an account (see page 2), create it under “Login” with your email and your licence number. An invite code from the club goes in right after you sign up.

In the hall, before the match

  1. Create the match. Signed in, you tap “SV match” and pick today's home game from the list; without an account you tap “New match” and load it from the schedule. The app fills in the game number, teams, date, hall and referees itself.
  2. Enter your licence number, because without it “Create match” stays greyed.
  3. Write down the game PIN. You need it if you have to continue the match on another device or restore it.
  4. Load both rosters. It is fastest when the team has sent its roster ahead and you only tap “Accept”. Otherwise you load the Einsatzliste with “Upload PDF” or type the players in. Then go through numbers, captain and liberos with the coach.
  5. At the coin toss, record sides and serve and have the captain and the coach of each team sign, on the screen or on their own phone. Set 1 starts only once all four have signed.

During the match

After each rally you tap the team that won the point. Substitutions, time-outs and sanctions have their own buttons, and you can undo a mistake or fix it on the correction board. When you are unsure, the “?” explains the screen you are on.

After the match

  1. The two captains sign first, then the scorer, the 2nd referee and the 1st referee.
  2. Tap “Confirm and approve” and leave the window open until the PDF is ready.
  3. “Close match” makes the match final and removes it from the device. If you want to keep a copy, save the PDF first.

If something goes wrong

The “?” on every screen shows the help for exactly that screen, with a short video and “Show me”. All questions and videos are on www.openvolley.app/en/support; there is a form there too, or you write to support@openvolley.app and get the answer by email.

If the device fails, a match scored with an account comes back on another device: tap “Restore match”, then enter the game number and the game PIN.

Should your scorers register?

An official match can be scored with or without an account. For everyone who scores regularly we recommend an account, because the match is then saved in the cloud as you go.

Club managers: set up once

The setup happens on manager.openvolley.app, with the same account as in the app. If “My Club” is missing there, write to support@openvolley.app so that an admin adds you.

  1. Invite your scorers. Under My Club › Invite Codes, “New invite code” makes a code for the role Scorer that works for up to 50 people and at most 90 days. Whoever enters it gets the role and your club at once; without a code, each sign-up waits under “Requests” for your approval.
  2. Under My Club › Licences, upload the licence lists for players, scorers and coaches as an .xlsx export. The file is read on your device, and only licence number, last name, first name and gender are kept.
  3. Send out the team links. Under My Club › Teams, “Create link” makes one link per team, which goes to the coach or the team manager.
  4. Check the club's contact email, because the final scoresheets of your games go to it.

Coaches and team managers open their team link and keep the season's squad up to date under “My Teams”. Before each game, away games included, they put the roster together with “Prepare roster”, mark the captain and the liberos and tap “Send roster”, ideally by the evening before. In the hall the scorer then only has to tap “Accept”.

Data protection

OpenVolley has no ads, no tracking and no analytics tools. The server is in Switzerland, at Infomaniak in Geneva; Cloudflare (USA) delivers the websites and forwards the traffic to the server.

A scoresheet holds the names, shirt numbers, licence numbers and signatures of players, team staff and officials. Of these, only the team names, the score and the shirt numbers are public, and for official matches the referees' names too. Licence numbers, signatures and dates of birth are never public.

Many players are minors. Whoever records their data (the club, team managers, scorers) is responsible for being allowed to do so and for informing the players and their parents. Match records are kept for their season and the two after it, then deleted. You can ask for access, correction or deletion at any time through support@openvolley.app; the full privacy policy is at www.openvolley.app/en/privacy.
