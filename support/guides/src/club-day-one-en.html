eScoresheet: day one for clubs

From your first home game you write the scoresheet on a tablet or laptop instead of on paper. This page says what scorers and club managers do on day one.

What you need

  • One device at the scorer's table. A tablet in landscape or a laptop. A phone works too, held upright.
  • The app. In the browser at app.openvolley.app, or as a desktop or Android app from get.openvolley.app. At the first open, choose “Swiss”.
  • Internet only to get started. The page must have loaded once, and the schedule needs a connection. Scoring itself works fully offline; the data goes to the server once the connection is back.
  • One practice evening. A test match with invented teams, before it counts. It is never saved on the server.

Scorers: day one

At home, once (20 minutes)

  1. Open the app and tap “?” › “Your first match”. The tour takes you through a test match: rosters, coin toss, set 1, set 5, signatures. The same is available as a video (7:29).
  2. Create an account under “Login” if your club has decided so (see page 2). You need your email and your licence number. If the club gave you an invite code, enter it right after signing up.

In the hall, before the match

  1. Create the match. Signed in: “SV match”, then pick today's home game from the list. Without an account: “New match”, then load the game from the schedule. The app fills in game number, teams, date, hall and referees.
  2. Enter your licence number. Without the scorer's licence number, “Create match” stays greyed.
  3. Write down the game PIN. You need it to continue the match on another device or to restore it.
  4. Load both rosters. Fastest: the team sent its roster ahead and you tap “Accept”. Otherwise “Upload PDF” with the Einsatzliste, or type the players in. Check numbers, captain and liberos with the coach.
  5. Coin toss and signatures. Record sides and serve. The captain and the coach of each team sign on the screen or on their own phone. Set 1 starts only once all four have signed.

During the match

After each rally you tap the team that wins the point. Substitutions, time-outs and sanctions have their own buttons. A mistake can be undone or fixed on the correction board. The “?” on every screen explains what you are looking at.

After the match

  1. The captains sign, then the scorer, the 2nd referee and the 1st referee.
  2. Tap “Confirm and approve” and leave the window open until the PDF is ready.
  3. “Close match”. Save the PDF first if you want a copy on the device.

If something goes wrong

  • In the app: the “?” on every screen, with a short video and “Show me”.
  • All questions and videos: www.openvolley.app/en/support
  • Questions and problems: the form on the support page, or support@openvolley.app. The answer comes by email.
  • The device fails: on another device tap “Restore match”, with game number and game PIN.

Should your scorers register?

An official match works with and without an account. We recommend an account for everyone who scores regularly, because the match is then saved in the cloud.

Club managers: set up once

Everything is on manager.openvolley.app, with the same account as in the app. If you don't see “My Club”, write to support@openvolley.app: an admin adds you.

  1. Invite your scorers. My Club › Invite Codes › “New invite code”: role Scorer, up to 50 people, valid up to 90 days. Whoever enters the code gets the role and your club at once. Without a code, each sign-up waits under “Requests” for your approval.
  2. Upload the licence lists. My Club › Licences: the .xlsx export of the licence lists for players, scorers and coaches. The file is read on your device; only licence number, last name, first name and gender are kept.
  3. Send the team links. My Club › Teams › “Create link”, one per team, to the coach or team manager.
  4. Check the contact email. It receives the final scoresheets of your games.

Team managers and coaches open the team link and keep the season's squad up to date under “My Teams”. Before each game: “Prepare roster”, mark captain and liberos, “Send roster”, ideally by the evening before. In the hall the scorer only taps “Accept”. This works for away games too.

Data protection

  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. The server is in Switzerland (Infomaniak, Geneva). Cloudflare (USA) delivers the websites and forwards the traffic to it.
  • What a scoresheet holds: names, shirt numbers, licence numbers and signatures of players, team staff and officials. Public are only team names, the score and shirt numbers, and the referees' names for official matches. Licence numbers, signatures and dates of birth are never public.
  • Minors: many players are minors. The club is responsible for being allowed to record their data and for informing players and parents.
  • Storage: match records are kept for their season and the two seasons after it, then deleted.
  • Your rights: access, correction or deletion at any time through support@openvolley.app. The full policy is at www.openvolley.app/en/privacy.
