eScoresheet: day one for scorers

From your club's first home game you write the scoresheet on a tablet or laptop instead of on paper. This page says what you do for that as a scorer, from the practice at home to the finished scoresheet.

For SVRZ games only. Mobiliar Cup and 1st League games are still scored on paper.

What you need

The scorer's table needs one device: a tablet in landscape or a laptop, or if need be a phone held upright. The app runs in the browser at app.openvolley.app; if you would rather install it, the desktop and Android apps are on get.openvolley.app. At the first open it asks how you use OpenVolley, and you choose “Swiss”.

You need internet only at the start, because the page has to load once and the schedule needs a connection. After that you can score with no network at all, and the data goes to the server once the connection is back.

At home, before the first match

  1. Open the app and tap “?”, then “Your first match”. The tour takes you through a test match, from the rosters and the coin toss through set 1 to set 5 and the signatures. If you would rather watch, the same exists as a video (7:29). A test match has invented teams and is never saved on the server.
  2. Ask your club whether you score with an account. If so, create it under “Login” with your email and your licence number; an invite code from the club goes in right after you sign up. With an account the match is saved in the cloud while you score, without one on this device only.

In the hall, before the match

  1. Create the match. Signed in, you tap “SV match” and pick today's home game from the list; without an account you tap “New match” and load it from the schedule. The app fills in the game number, teams, date, hall and referees itself.
  2. Enter your licence number, because without it “Create match” stays greyed. Ask the referees for their email address and type it in with their names: the finished scoresheet goes to it.
  3. Write down the game PIN. You need it if you have to continue the match on another device or restore it.
  4. Load both rosters. It is fastest when the team has sent its roster ahead and you only tap “Accept”. Otherwise you load the Einsatzliste with “Upload PDF” or type the players in. Then go through numbers, captain and liberos with the coach.
  5. At the coin toss, record sides and serve and have the captain and the coach of each team sign, on the screen or on their own phone. Set 1 starts only once all four have signed.

During the match

After each rally you tap the team that won the point. Substitutions, time-outs and sanctions have their own buttons, and you can undo a mistake or fix it on the correction board. When you are unsure, the “?” explains the screen you are on.

After the match

  1. The two captains sign first, then the scorer, the 2nd referee and the 1st referee.
  2. Tap “Confirm and approve” and leave the window open until the PDF is ready. A few minutes later the scoresheet goes by email as a PDF to the referees and to each team's contact.
  3. “Close match” makes the match final and removes it from the device. If you want to keep a copy, save the PDF first.

If something goes wrong

The “?” on every screen shows the help for exactly that screen, with a short video and “Show me”. All questions and videos are on www.openvolley.app/en/support; there is a form there too, or you write to support@openvolley.app and get the answer by email.

If the device fails, a match scored with an account comes back on another device: tap “Restore match”, then enter the game number and the game PIN.
