# Delete your account

**OpenVolley** · Version 1.0 · As of 16 November 2026

This page explains how to delete your OpenVolley account and the data that goes with it. OpenVolley is run by Luca Canepa as a private person (see the [legal notice](impressum.md)).

## 1. In the app or on the website

1. Open OpenVolley (the Android app, the desktop app or `manager.openvolley.app`) and sign in.
2. Open your profile (your name at the top).
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm with **Delete my account**.

The deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. It takes effect immediately.

## 2. By email

If you cannot sign in, write to **support@openvolley.app** from the email address of the account and say that you want the account deleted. I may ask for proof of your identity, and I answer within 30 days.

## 3. What is deleted

- Your account and profile (name, email address, country, date of birth, roles).
- Your sign-ins and your password hash.
- Your My matches list and your editor rights on matches.
- Your cloud backups and logs, and your access to the scoresheets you uploaded.

Encrypted server backups keep a copy until they expire, after about 7 months at most. If one of them is ever restored, I delete your account again.

## 4. What is kept

- The matches you scored and their scoresheets. They are the official match records of the clubs and the association, and your name stays on them as the scorer, without your account.
- Matches saved on your own device stay on it. Delete the app data or uninstall the app to remove them.

You can also ask for access, correction or deletion of other data (for example of a player on a roster) at **support@openvolley.app**. More in the [privacy policy](privacy.md), section 19.
